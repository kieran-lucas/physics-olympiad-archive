{\Large\textbf{Three balls}}

Three small identical balls (denoted as A, B, and C) of mass m each are
connected with two massless rods of length $l$ so that one of the rods connects
the balls A and B, and the other rod connects the balls B and C. The connection
at the ball B is hinged, and the angle between the rods can change
effortlessly. The system rests in weightlessness so that all the balls lie on
one line. The ball A is given instantaneously a velocity perpendicular to the
rods. Find the minimal distance d between the balls A and C during the
subsequent motion of the system. Any friction is to be neglected.
