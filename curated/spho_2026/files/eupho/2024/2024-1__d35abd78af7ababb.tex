\textbf{T1: Sliding puck (10 pts)}

A puck (a small disc) with radius $r$ and uniform density is moving on a
horizontal plane with the velocity $v_0$ without rotation. The puck meets a
fixed half-circular wall with a radius $R \gg r$ and starts to move along the
wall. The coefficient of friction with the wall is $\mu$, and friction with the
horizontal plane is negligible.

\begin{center}\includegraphics[width=0.27\linewidth]{figures/EuPhO_2024_Q1_fig1.png}\end{center}

a) (8 pts) Find the velocity of the puck $v_e$ when it leaves the wall.

b) (2 pts) Sketch the graph $v_e(\mu)$. Indicate important features of the
graph. You are encouraged to sketch the graph even if you haven't found the
exact formula for $v_e$.
