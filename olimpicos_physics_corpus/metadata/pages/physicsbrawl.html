Physics Brawl Problems and Solutions

An online team competition run by FYKOS from 2012, at its peak drawing 1,574 teams from 81 countries. Teams of five had three hours, starting with seven problems and unlocking more as they solved. The organisers ended it in 2025, citing the use of AI.
