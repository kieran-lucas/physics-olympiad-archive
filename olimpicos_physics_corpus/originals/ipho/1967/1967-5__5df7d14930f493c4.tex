The following devices and materials are given:
\begin{enumerate}\itemsep0pt
  \item Balance (without weights)
  \item Calorimeter
  \item Thermometer
  \item Source of voltage
  \item Switches
  \item Wires
  \item Electric heater
  \item Stop-watch
  \item Beakers
  \item Water
  \item Petroleum
  \item Sand (for balancing)
\end{enumerate}
Determine specific heat of petroleum. The specific heat of water is
$1~\mathrm{cal/(g\cdot{}^\circ C)}$. The specific heat of the calorimeter is
$0.092~\mathrm{cal/(g\cdot{}^\circ C)}$.

Discuss assumptions made in the solution.
